    artefact(s): LeNet-1
     
    Physical installation translating LeNet-1 — the first convolutional neural network (Bell Labs, 1989) — from matrices to copper and from scalar activations to brightness. 1.63m frame of transparent flex PCBs, WS2812B LEDs, low-iron glass, Raspberry Pi 4. A handwritten digit is fed in every 10 seconds and propagated layer by layer through the physical network. Co-built with Nick Oh as socius labs.
